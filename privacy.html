Privacy Policy for Moreland Games

Effective date: November 24, 2026

This policy covers the free browser games published by Moreland Games: Unlikely Flyers and Farm House Run. Moreland Games is run by Trey Moreland, an individual developer in Houston, Texas.

What we collect

Nothing. The games have no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. They do not collect your name, email, location or any other personal information.

What is saved on your device

Each game saves your best scores and the levels you have unlocked, so your progress is still there the next time you play. This is kept in your browser's local storage on your own device. It is never sent to Trey Moreland or anyone else.

You can delete this saved progress at any time by clearing your browser's site data for the game.

Hosting

The games are played at MorelandGames.com. Like most websites, the company that hosts the site may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and browser type, when a page loads. Hosts generally use these logs to keep a site running and secure. Moreland Games does not use them to identify or track players.

Sounds

The games use CC0 (public domain) sound clips. They are stored with the game files, so playing them does not contact any outside service.

Children

The games are safe for players of any age, since no personal information is collected from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
